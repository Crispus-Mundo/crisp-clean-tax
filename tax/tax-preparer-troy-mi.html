Quick answers

Who do you prepare returns for?

Individuals, seniors, self-employed people and small businesses for Troy residents and businesses, including clients with income or family in other countries. We also file extensions and build year-round tax plans.

How do I get started?

Pick your return type, read and sign a short notice, and pay your starting fee online. Your secure client portal login arrives in about 5 minutes.

Do I have to come into an office?

No. You upload your documents in your secure client portal, and we work with you by phone, text and email.

Is my tax information kept confidential?

Yes. Tax information you share is kept confidential under IRC §7216 and is never used or disclosed without your written consent.

Can you file an extension?

Yes. Choose "Extension" when you get started.

Do you do year-round tax planning?

Yes. We prepare tax plans for individuals and business owners, not just the yearly return.

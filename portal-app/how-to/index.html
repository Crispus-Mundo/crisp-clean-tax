Suggestions: ideas and next steps, made for you

Cyars can now send you ideas, notices and next steps right inside your portal. You read the full write-up, tap the links, and tell us what you think, all in one place.

1 minute, no sound needed: every step is written on screen.

How to use it

  1. Get the app. Open the app link on your phone or computer and tap Install. iPhone: Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen.
  2. Sign in once. Last name + your 4-digit PIN. On your own device keep "Keep me signed in" checked so the app opens straight to your portal. Leave it unchecked on a shared computer.
  3. Look for "New from Cyars" at the top of your Home page. You also get an email with the type of notice and a short summary.
  4. Open Suggestions to see them all, newest first.
  5. Read the full write-up: what it is, why it may fit you, how it works and what we need from you. Money details stay in the portal, never in email.
  6. Tell us what you think: "I am interested", "Not now" or "I have a question". Cyars gets your answer right away.
  7. Sign out any time from the top of any page, or in Account & logins › Stay signed in › Sign out on all devices.

The workflow

Every return goes through four steps. Each step hands drafts to the next. A RED flag sends a line back to Prepare.

Bookkeeping

Upload, review lines, reconcile one month at a time, then Send to tax return. Skip it for W-2/1099-only clients.

Prepare

Check the preflight, Pull portal documents, press Prepare, then verify every line against its document.

Andrew review

Press Review, open the Review Desk, tie each line to its source, work the 13-item checklist.

Handoff

When the check passes: Build handoff package, key lines into SuccessTax, update the Season Desk.

Full written steps: Crisp Clean Tax Workflow — Trial Guide.

Payroll

How your hours become your pay. Your rate is on your milestone record; your pay period is confirmed by Cyars in writing.

▶ Watch: hours to pay · 18 sec
  1. Clock in and out in Homebase. Homebase is the record of your hours.
  2. Post your standup at the start of every work day. No standup = that day didn't happen for audit purposes.
  3. Check your timesheet before the period closes. Missed a punch? Tell Cyars in writing with the real time — don't guess.
  4. Cyars reviews and approves your hours against your standups.
  5. You get paid. Pay details and stubs live in Gusto.

Pay questions go to Cyars in writing so they can be answered in writing.

Ground rules

You may

  • Upload, run Prepare and Review, verify lines, add questions
  • Fix bookkeeping lines and reconcile statements
  • Draft plans and research memos (they stay DRAFT)
  • Ask Cyars in writing when something looks wrong

You may not

  • Send anything to a client — Cyars approves every client message first
  • Change a client's consent or turn AI on without consent
  • Sign off a return without a verified credential on file
  • Copy client documents or numbers outside these apps

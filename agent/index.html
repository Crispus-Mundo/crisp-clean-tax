Typing into SuccessTax happens on a computer

A phone can't type into SuccessTax. This app shows which returns are ready, follows the agent live, and lets you pause or stop it. The typing itself is done by the Crispus Mundo Tax Agent Chrome extension on your computer.

  1. Open SuccessTax in Chrome and open the client's return.
  2. Click the gold CM button in Chrome's toolbar.
  3. Sign in, pick the return, press Start, and watch.
